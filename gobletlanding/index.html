One number, and it is about you

Every wine gets a score out of 10 answering a single question: would you buy it again? Not what a critic thought, not what it costs. A celebrated bottle that does not suit your palate scores low — and that is the whole point.

Questions

How does Goblet know what I like?

You rate wines out of 10 on one question — would you buy it again? Goblet learns from those ratings, and every wine you score sharpens the next prediction. One rating is enough to start.

Do I need to know anything about wine?

No. That is rather the point. Goblet explains each pick in plain language and in terms of wines you already told it you liked.

Is it free?

Yes, with a monthly allowance of scans. Goblet Premium lifts the cap for people who scan often.

What does the score actually mean?

Taste compatibility — the chance you personally would enjoy the glass. Never critic scores, prestige or price. A famous bottle that does not suit you scores low, and it should.
